2. Concorde is a platform

Concorde helps people find each other and organise sport. We are not a club, a coach or a sports school.

  • Courts are owned and run by clubs. Lessons and academy groups are given by coaches and clubs.
  • Agreements to play, book a court or take a lesson are between the players, parents, coaches and clubs involved.
  • We don't control and aren't responsible for the quality, safety or availability of courts, lessons or games, or for what users do on and off court.

3. Accounts

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. Children use Kids Academy through a parent's or legal guardian's account.
  • Use your real name and a phone number you control. Keep your details up to date.
  • One person, one account. Don't share your account or sign up on behalf of someone else (except a parent adding their child).
  • You are responsible for what happens on your account. Tell us quickly if you think someone else is using it.
  • Clubs and coaches who register must have the right to represent that club or to offer those lessons.

4. How to behave

Concorde works because people show up and play fair. You agree not to:

  • harass, threaten, insult or discriminate against anyone, in chat or on court;
  • post false, misleading, offensive or illegal content;
  • pretend to be someone else or misrepresent your level on purpose;
  • use Concorde to sell, advertise or spam, unless we've agreed to it;
  • collect other users' data, or use their contact details for anything other than organising sport;
  • try to break, copy, reverse-engineer or overload the app.

Please report any behaviour that worries you. We may remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules.

5. Games, court requests and lessons

  • Open games: if you host a game, describe it honestly (time, place, level). If you join one, show up or cancel as early as you can.
  • Court requests: a court request is only confirmed once the club accepts it in the app. The club's own rules, prices and cancellation terms apply to the court.
  • Lessons and groups: requests to join a group or book a private lesson are confirmed by the coach. The coach decides on content, level and schedule.
  • Levels and ladders: levels and ladder positions are a guide to help find fair games. They are not an official ranking.

6. Kids Academy

  • Only a parent or legal guardian may add a child, and they confirm they have the right to do so.
  • The parent is responsible for the child's participation, for telling the coach about any health or safety needs, and for bringing and collecting the child as agreed with the coach or club.
  • Coaches and clubs are responsible for supervising children during their sessions and for following applicable safeguarding rules.

7. Clubs and coaches

  • Concorde is free during early access. Any fees for clubs or coaches are announced in advance and shown in the app before they apply.

  • Clubs and coaches are responsible for their own facilities, staff, insurance, qualifications, permits and taxes.

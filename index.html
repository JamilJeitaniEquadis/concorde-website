FAQ

Quick answers

Is it free for clubs?

Yes. Clubs use Concorde for free: calendar, opening hours, court requests, kids programs and the earnings view.

Is Concorde free for players?

Yes, there's a free plan. Concorde Pro is an optional subscription for players who want more. Pro pricing is announced at launch.

How do parents pay?

Per session, inside the app. Whish and card payments are coming at launch. Card details are handled by the payment provider, never stored by Concorde.

Which areas in Lebanon?

We're starting in Beirut and Metn, and opening more areas as players join. Tell us where you play on WhatsApp and we'll let you know when your area is on.

Which sports can I play?

Tennis and padel are live. Basketball and more sports are coming soon.

When can I download the app?

The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon. Message us on WhatsApp to get early access.
